People choose a lawyer, counselor or care provider on trust. They read your site, check your reviews and often ask an AI assistant before they ever call. And your team loses hours to intake calls, scheduling and reminders.

<!--more-->

## Get found: [Digital Entity]({{< relref "/services/digital-entity" >}})

- A clear, professional website: your practice areas or services, your team and how to get started
- Accurate profiles on Google and the directories your clients use, so AI assistants describe you correctly

## Answer first: [AI Tools]({{< relref "/services/ai-tools" >}})

- An AI assistant that answers general questions (hours, location, services, insurance or fee basics) and books consultations
- Set up to collect only the intake details you choose, and never to give legal, medical or counseling advice

## Less admin: [Operations Automation]({{< relref "/services/operations-automation" >}})

- Appointment reminders and follow-ups that cut no-shows
- Intake forms and paperwork that land organized, ready for your team

## Keep growing: [Development Management]({{< relref "/services/development-management" >}})

- Review requests and reputation monitoring, within your profession's rules on testimonials

## The goal

More of the right clients booking with you, and less front-office time on the phone.
