Glow Wild is a WILD LLC company based in Parker, Colorado ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through glowilds.com (the "site") and what we do with it.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** When you fill in our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, business name, phone number, website, the service you are interested in, and your message. We also receive whatever you send when you email or call us.

**Basic technical data.** Like any website, the server that hosts the site records standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. Our hosting provider uses this data to deliver and protect the site.

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

## How we use it

We use your information only to:

- reply to your request and give you the free business check-up you asked for,
- provide and bill for services you hire us for,
- keep basic business records and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers that process information for us:

- **Formspree** receives contact-form submissions and forwards them to our inbox.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site and processes the technical data described above.
- **Our email provider** stores the messages we send and receive.

These providers may only use your information to perform their service for us. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## How long we keep it

We keep contact requests and business correspondence for as long as we need them to work with you and to meet our tax and legal record-keeping duties. After that we delete them.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at [hello@glowilds.com](mailto:hello@glowilds.com) and we will respond within 30 days.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and the site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The site is meant for business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date shown at the top.
