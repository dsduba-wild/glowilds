## One-time setup or ongoing management?

Most work starts with a **one-time setup**: we build it, connect it, show you how it works and hand it over. It's yours, and it keeps running.

**Ongoing management** is for what needs attention every month: asking for and replying to reviews, posting on social media, monthly reports, and fixes and improvements as things change.

| Compare | One-time setup | Ongoing management |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | We build it and hand it over | We keep it working and growing |
| Examples | Website, Google profile, AI receptionist, booking and reminder texts | Reviews, social posts, monthly reports, new tools |
| Who runs it after | You, with the tools we set up | Us, with you approving the important parts |

You can start with a setup and add ongoing management later, or stop it any time. See the [price ranges](/#pricing); Sergei goes over your exact price after your free check-up.
