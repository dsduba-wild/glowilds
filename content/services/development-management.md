Getting set up is the start. Growth comes from what happens every month after: new reviews coming in, social media that stays active, numbers you can actually read, and steady improvements. We stay on as your growth partner and handle that for you, adjusting as customers and AI keep changing.

## Why it keeps paying off

- **Reviews:** customers and Google trust recent reviews more than old ones, so new ones need to keep coming in.
- **Social media:** an account that stopped posting months ago looks like a business that closed. Regular posts show you're open and busy.
- **Reporting:** each month you see what brought in calls and bookings, so you spend time and money on what works.
- **Improvements:** small fixes add up month after month, and new tools get added when they're worth it.

## What we manage

1. Automatic review requests after each job, with one-tap links to your Google review page
2. AI-drafted replies to reviews in your voice that you approve before posting
3. Monthly check-ins on calls, direction requests, website clicks and bookings
4. Posting managed on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and your Google profile, so your business and brand look consistent and professional everywhere
5. A simple plan for the next step: new services, new areas or new tools
