About Glow Wild

Helping local businesses stay visible and run smarter as the world moves to AI.

Built by Someone Who Has Done the Work

Glow Wild is the digital arm of WILD LLC. Our founder has worked every position in a small business — from the floor all the way to CEO — so we know what a busy owner’s day actually looks like. We build tools that fit into that day, not ones that add to it.

Plain English, No Lock-In

No jargon and no long contracts. Your website, domain, Google profile and data stay in your name. If you ever want to move on, you take everything with you.

Let Your Business Glow

The AI era is changing how customers find and choose local businesses. We’re here to make sure yours doesn’t get left in the dark.
