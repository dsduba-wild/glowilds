AI Front Desk

An AI helper that answers customers 24/7 — even when you are on a job.

Every missed call is a job that goes to someone else. We set up an AI assistant trained on your business that responds right away on your website chat, texts and missed calls — then hands the details to you.

What it can do

  1. Answer common questions: prices, service area, hours, what you do and don’t do
  2. Collect job details and photos so you can quote faster
  3. Book appointments straight into your calendar
  4. Text back missed calls within seconds
  5. Send you a clean summary of every conversation

You decide what it’s allowed to say, and you can step in any time.
