Your business needs to exist online as one clear, consistent "entity": the same name, services, area and reputation everywhere customers and AI assistants look. We build that for you, starting with a fast website and connecting everything to it.

## What you get

1. A fast, mobile-first website with your services, service area, photos and reviews
2. The right structure: clear pages, headings and behind-the-scenes business data (schema) that search engines and AI can read
3. SEO basics done right: titles, descriptions, speed, sitemap and a secure connection
4. Your Google Business Profile created or claimed, verified and filled in properly
5. Matching name, address and phone across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and other directories
6. Periodic checks of how AI assistants describe your business, and fixes when they get it wrong
7. Your own domain and accounts: you own everything

We can upgrade what you have instead of starting over: speed it up, fix the structure and mobile layout, and connect it to your listings.
