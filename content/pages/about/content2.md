Hi, I’m Sergei. I’ve spent 10 years working every job in business, from the ground floor up to CEO, so I know what it takes to run one day to day.

I formed WILD LLC to buy a business, but at the final signing, with a six-figure check on the table, I spotted a licensing risk and walked away. A year later the license was gone and the buyer lost everything. I kept the company. It became home to my eBay store, Wild Alice (300+ sales, 99.4% positive feedback), and now to Glow Wild, its digital arm here in Parker, Colorado.

I designed the logo myself: a glowing triangle that bends into a “G.” That glow is the job. I help local businesses shine where people look, on Google and in AI. I do the homework first, set everything up, explain it in plain English, and stick around to keep it working.
