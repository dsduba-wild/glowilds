Cleaning businesses live on repeat customers and referrals — and lose hours every week to scheduling texts and quote back-and-forth.

## What we set up

- A website with clear packages and an instant quote form (bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency)
- Online booking and automatic reminders that cut down on no-shows
- An AI assistant that answers "Do you bring supplies?" and "Do you do move-outs?" for you
- Review requests after every clean to keep your rating strong

## The goal

A fuller calendar of recurring clients with less time spent on your phone.
