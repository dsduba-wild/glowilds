Every call you miss on a ladder is a job that goes to whoever answers first, and every quote you write at 10 p.m. is time taken from your family. We catch the calls, speed up the quotes and clear the paperwork, so you win more jobs and get your evenings back.

## Get found: [Digital Entity]({{< relref "/services/digital-entity" >}})

- A website with a project gallery, service area and a quote form that asks for photos up front
- A Google Business Profile that shows up when people nearby search for your trade, in every neighborhood you serve

## Answer first: [AI Integration]({{< relref "/services/ai-integration" >}})

- Instant text-back for every missed call
- An AI assistant that answers "Do you bring supplies?" or "Can you haul a couch today?" and books estimates or pickups

## Less admin: [Operations Automation]({{< relref "/services/operations-automation" >}})

- "Send a photo, get a price range" quotes, online booking and automatic reminders that cut no-shows
- Receipts and payments logged by photo or text

## Keep growing: [Development Management]({{< relref "/services/development-management" >}})

- Review requests when each job wraps up, and repeat-service reminders for cleaning and maintenance clients

## Common questions

### Do I need a new website to start?

No. We can start with your Google Business Profile and missed-call text-back, and add a website when it makes sense. If you already have one, we improve it instead of starting over.

### What happens when I miss a call on a job?

The caller gets a text right away and can describe the job and send photos. You see the details when you're off the ladder, and the lead doesn't move on to the next contractor.

### Who sets the prices in my quotes?

You do. Quotes are built from your prices and wait for your OK before they go to a customer.

## The goal

More jobs won, fewer leads lost while your hands are full, and evenings that belong to you again.
