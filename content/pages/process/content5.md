### Does the check-up cost anything or commit me to anything?

No. It's free, with no obligation, and the list of findings is yours to keep either way.

### Who will I work with?

Sergei Dubovik, the founder, from the first call. He does the research, explains things in plain English and stays on after launch to keep things working.

### Do I have to do everything at once?

No. You can start with one fix from the check-up and add more later. You can also start with a setup, add ongoing management later, or stop it any time.

### Who owns what you build?

You do. Your website, accounts and data are set up in your name, not ours.
