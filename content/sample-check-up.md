**This is an example.** "Maple Street Bakery" is made up. Your check-up looks at your own business the same way and comes back in the same plain format.

## What we checked

- How the business shows up when people search on Google, Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yelp and AI assistants such as ChatGPT
- The website: speed on a phone, clear pages, contact details and the behind-the-scenes business data search engines read
- Calls, messages and bookings: what happens when the owner can't pick up
- Reviews: how many, how recent, and whether they get replies
- The weekly admin: what takes the owner's time and could run on its own

## What we found

| Area | What we saw | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | Google shows one number, Yelp and the website another | Customers call a line nobody answers |
| Hours | Google still shows last year's holiday hours | People skip a visit they think is closed |
| AI assistants | One assistant lists the wrong street | AI answers are now where many people look first |
| Website | Takes about 6 seconds to load on a phone; no menu page | Visitors leave before they see what's for sale |
| Missed calls | Calls during the morning rush go to a full voicemail | Each missed call can be a lost order |
| Reviews | 41 reviews, the newest from 5 months ago, no replies | Recent, answered reviews build trust |
| Admin | About 4 hours a week confirming custom-cake orders by phone | Time that could go to baking or selling |

## What to fix first

1. **Make the phone number and hours the same everywhere.** Start with Google, then Apple Maps, Yelp and the website. Small job, biggest effect.
2. **Text back missed calls.** A missed call gets a text within seconds and can book a pickup time.
3. **Ask for reviews after each custom order,** and reply to every review.
4. **Speed up the website and add the menu.**
5. **Confirm custom-cake orders by text** instead of by phone.
