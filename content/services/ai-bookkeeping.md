Most small business owners know their money is "somewhere" between the register, the bank, Venmo and a stack of receipts. We set up a simple AI helper that does the logging for you.

## How it works

1. Snap a photo of a receipt, invoice or cash count — or just text a note like "sold $240 cash"
2. The AI reads it and logs it as cash or card, income or expense
3. Everything lands in one organized sheet you can actually understand
4. Get a weekly cash-flow summary: what came in, what went out, what's left
5. Hand your bookkeeper or accountant clean records at tax time

No new software to learn — you use the phone you already carry.
