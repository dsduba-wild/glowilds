Your business needs to exist online as one clear, consistent "entity": the same name, services, area and reputation everywhere customers and AI assistants look. We build that for you, starting with a fast, custom website and connecting everything to it.

1. A fast, mobile-first website designed around your business, not copied and pasted from someone else's, with your services, service area, photos and reviews
2. The right structure: clear pages, headings and behind-the-scenes business data (schema) that search engines and AI can read
3. SEO basics done right: titles, descriptions, speed, sitemap and a secure connection
4. Your Google Business Profile created or claimed, verified and filled in properly
5. Matching name, address and phone across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and other directories
6. Business accounts on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and other platforms, created or cleaned up and connected to your website, with the same name, logo and look
7. Periodic checks of how AI assistants describe your business, and fixes when they get it wrong

## Common questions

### What is a "digital entity"?

It's how Google, maps and AI assistants understand your business: one name, one set of services, one area and one reputation. When those details match everywhere, they can describe and show your business correctly.

### Who owns the website and accounts?

You do. Your domain, website, Google profile and social accounts are set up in your name, not ours.
