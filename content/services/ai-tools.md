Every missed call is a job that goes to someone else. We set up AI tools trained on your business that respond right away on your website chat, texts and missed calls, then hand the details to you.

## What they can do

1. Answer common questions: prices, service area, hours, what you do and don't do
2. Collect job details and photos so you can quote faster
3. Book appointments straight into your calendar
4. Text back missed calls within seconds
5. Send you a clean summary of every conversation

You decide what the AI is allowed to say, and you can step in any time.
