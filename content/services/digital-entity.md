2. The right structure: clear pages, headings and behind-the-scenes business data (schema) that search engines and AI can read
3. SEO basics done right: titles, descriptions, speed, sitemap and a secure connection
4. Your Google Business Profile created or claimed, verified and filled in properly
5. Matching name, address and phone across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and other directories
6. Business accounts on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and other platforms, created or cleaned up and connected to your website, with the same name, logo and look
7. Periodic checks of how AI assistants describe your business, and fixes when they get it wrong
8. Your own domain and accounts: you own everything
