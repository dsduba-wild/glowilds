Local Shops & Studios

Retail shops, hobby stores, studios and neighborhood favorites.

Local shops win on community and personality — but online, they’re competing with big-box stores and marketplaces.

What we set up

  • A website upgrade that shows off your store, events and what makes you different
  • Google and Apple Maps listings with up-to-date hours, photos and products
  • AI money tracking: log cash and card sales by photo or text, get a weekly cash-flow picture
  • Event and new-arrival posts that keep regulars coming back

The goal

More walk-ins from nearby searches, and a clear view of where your money goes.
