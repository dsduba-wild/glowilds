Google & Local Search

Show up on the map when people nearby search for what you do.

When someone searches “junk removal near me” or “house cleaners,” the businesses in the map results get most of the calls. We set up and tune your Google Business Profile and local listings so you’re one of them.

What we handle

  1. Create or claim your Google Business Profile and get it verified
  2. Categories, services, service area, hours and photos done right
  3. Consistent name, address and phone across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and other directories
  4. Regular posts and updates so your profile stays active
  5. Simple monthly check-ins on calls, direction requests and website clicks
