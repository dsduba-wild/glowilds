Nonprofits run on small teams and volunteers who already give more than they have. We make it easy for people to find your hours, events and how to help, and we take the phone calls and spreadsheets off your plate, so your people spend their time on the mission instead of burning out.

<!--more-->

## Get found: [Digital Entity]({{< relref "/services/digital-entity" >}})

- A clear website with your mission, programs, events and easy donate, volunteer or join buttons
- Accurate Google and map listings, so people and AI assistants find your hours and locations

## Answer first: [AI Integration]({{< relref "/services/ai-integration" >}})

- An AI assistant that answers "When is the pantry open?", "Where do I drop off donations?" or "How do I join the chamber?" any time
- Volunteer and member sign-ups collected and sent to the right person

## Less admin: [Operations Automation]({{< relref "/services/operations-automation" >}})

- Volunteer scheduling, event registration and reminders that send themselves
- Donations, membership renewals and receipts logged and organized

## Keep growing: [Development Management]({{< relref "/services/development-management" >}})

- Event and campaign posts, reviews and member stories that build trust
- Simple numbers on reach, sign-ups and donations for your board and grant reports

## Common questions

### We run mostly on volunteers. Who keeps it running?

We set it up to run on its own, show your team how it works in plain English, and can stay on to keep it working. There's no new software to learn.

### Can it help with board and grant reports?

Yes. Reach, sign-ups and donations are collected as you go, so a report doesn't start from a blank page.

### Do you work with chambers and associations, not just charities?

Yes. Chambers of commerce, associations, faith groups, food banks and community centers all fit.

## The goal

More people served, more volunteers and members, and time back for your staff, for the mission and for themselves.
