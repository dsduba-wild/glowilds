Local shops win on community and personality. Online, they're competing with big-box stores and marketplaces.

## Get found: [Digital Entity](/services/digital-entity/)

- A website that shows off your store, events and what makes you different
- Google and Apple Maps listings with up-to-date hours, photos and products

## Answer first: [AI Tools](/services/ai-tools/)

- An AI assistant that answers "Are you open Sunday?" and "Do you carry this?" day and night

## Less admin: [Operations Automation](/services/operations-automation/)

- Cash and card sales logged by photo or text, with a weekly cash-flow picture

## Keep growing: [Development Management](/services/development-management/)

- Event and new-arrival posts that keep regulars coming back, plus a steady flow of fresh reviews

## The goal

More walk-ins from nearby searches, and a clear view of where your money goes.
