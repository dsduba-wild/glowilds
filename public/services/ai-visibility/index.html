AI Search Visibility

Be the business AI assistants recommend.

More and more people skip the search results and just ask an AI assistant: “Who’s a good painter near me?” Those assistants pull from websites, listings and reviews. If your information is thin, messy or missing, you don’t get mentioned.

How we get you in the answer

  1. Clear, well-structured website content that spells out what you do, where and for whom
  2. Behind-the-scenes business data (schema) that machines can read
  3. Accurate, matching information across every listing AI tools draw from
  4. A steady flow of fresh reviews that describe your real work
  5. Periodic checks of how AI assistants describe your business — and fixes when they get it wrong
