Most small business owners lose hours every week to admin, and know their money is "somewhere" between the register, the bank, Venmo and a stack of receipts. We automate the repetitive parts so you can get back to the work.

## What we automate

1. Money tracking: snap a photo of a receipt or text "sold $240 cash", and the AI logs it as income or expense
2. A weekly cash-flow summary: what came in, what went out, what's left
3. Scheduling, reminders and follow-up texts that send themselves
4. Quotes and invoices created from job details, ready for you to approve
5. Clean records for your bookkeeper or accountant at tax time

No new software to learn: you use the phone you already carry.
