When we set up a website, an AI helper or an automation for you, we work with your business information: customer messages, bookings, receipts and account logins. Here's how we treat it.

## It stays yours

You own your website, your accounts and your data. We set things up in your name, not ours.

## We use it only for your work

We use your data to do the work you hired us for, and nothing else. We don't sell it, rent it or share it for advertising.

## Access you control

We ask only for the access a job needs. Where a service allows it, you add us as a user instead of sharing your password, and you can remove us at any time.

## AI that follows your rules

You decide what an AI helper may say and do, and you can step in at any time. Before we connect a tool, we tell you which outside services it uses and what information it sends them.

## Money and records

Automations can log receipts and sales and summarize your cash flow. This is admin help, not accounting or tax advice: your bookkeeper or accountant still keeps your books.

## When we stop working together

Everything stays with you. We remove our access and hand over anything you still need.

## Visitors to this website

How we handle information sent through glowilds.com, such as the contact form, is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
